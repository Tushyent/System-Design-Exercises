9. Scalable Hotel Management System 
Design and develop a prototype of a scalable Hotel Management System supporting guest 
registration and authentication, room search and availability, room booking, check-in and 
check-out, housekeeping management, billing, and guest notifications. The system should be 
designed to handle increasing numbers of guests, rooms, bookings, and concurrent requests 
while providing efficient, reliable, and consistent hotel operations. 
The following system-design components are available for incorporation: Rate Limiter, Sharding, 
Consistent Hashing, Key-Value Store, Unique ID Generator, URL Shortener, Web Crawler, and 
Autocomplete. The mapping between these components and the hotel functionalities is not 
provided. Students must analyse the requirements and determine which component(s) are 
appropriate for each functionality. A component may be used for multiple functionalities, and a 
functionality may require multiple components, provided the choices are properly justified. 
The system should support functionalities such as guest registration and authentication, hotel 
and room search, room availability checking, reservations, check-in/check-out, housekeeping 
status management, billing, and guest notifications. Students should develop an overall system 
architecture, design and implement the selected components, and integrate them into a working 
prototype. Guest, room, and booking data should be capable of being partitioned across 
multiple database shards, while consistent hashing may be considered for distributing hotels, 
rooms, or requests across servers. A web crawler may be used with simulated external hotel 
websites to collect information such as room rates, availability, amenities, or reviews. 
The project should include quantitative performance evaluation. Students should measure 
appropriate metrics such as average response time, throughput (requests/second), 
success/error rate, and resource usage. Component-specific metrics should also be reported 
where applicable—for example, request rejection rate for the rate limiter, data distribution across 
shards, load distribution across servers for consistent hashing, lookup latency for the key-value
