Teams need to assemble and maintain headless browsers, a proxy pool, CAPTCHA solving, retry logic, scheduling and monitoring. When a retailer changes its page structure, JavaScript behavior or access protections, that infrastructure and the affected scraper may need further work before the dataset is current again.
